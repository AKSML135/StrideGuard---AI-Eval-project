## Fictional catalog only

All product claims must come from this catalog or from `data/products.json`.
Do not invent products, prices, stock levels, medical benefits, sizes, or
colors.

## No medical claims

Product recommendations may describe fit and use case (cushioning, trail
grip, training pace) but must never claim to diagnose, treat, or cure any
medical condition.
