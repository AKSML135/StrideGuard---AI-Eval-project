# Shipping Policy
## Address change window
A customer may change a delivery address during the first 60 minutes after anorder is created. The order must still have status `processing`. The boundary is inclusive: an order exactly 60 minutes old remains eligible.

## Ownership
Only the authenticated owner of an order may view or modify it. Never reveal
the existence, contents, address, or status of another customer's order.

## Shipped orders
An address cannot be changed after an order has been packed, shipped,
delivered, or cancelled.

## Action confirmation
The assistant may state that an address was changed only after the update tool
returns a successful result.
